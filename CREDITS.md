## Replacing an image

Drop a new file into `src/assets/`, point the import at it, and rebuild. Astro regenerates the
responsive AVIF/WebP variants automatically. Update the row above so the record stays accurate.

## If you commission your own photography

Real photographs of the team, and of the plants you actually recruit into, will outperform
stock every time on a trust-driven site like this. When you have them, these are the highest
value slots to replace first:

1. The About hero — a real team photo is the single biggest credibility upgrade on the site
2. The homepage hero
3. The two role heroes
