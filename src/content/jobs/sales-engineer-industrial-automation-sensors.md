Diamond Recruiters is running this search for a European-headquartered manufacturer of industrial sensors, connectivity and factory-automation components with an established US sales organization.

Territories are open in the **Southeast, Upper Midwest, Mountain West and parts of the Northeast**. One hire per patch.

## Who this is for

You can walk a production floor, work out why the sensing is failing, and close the fix. You know where sensing sits in a control architecture and you can hold a conversation with a controls engineer about it.

Territory ownership with real autonomy — and the quota that comes with it.

## The work

- Own a defined territory of OEM and end-user accounts across factory automation, robotics and industrial connectivity
- Diagnose application requirements on site and turn them into product recommendations: sensing, connectivity, safety, control components
- Run the full cycle — prospecting, technical qualification, quoting, close — alongside regional applications engineering
- Build and hold the distributor relationships in your patch
- Forecast a pipeline in CRM that survives contact with a review meeting

## What you need

- **3+ years technical sales** in industrial automation, sensors or plant-floor control systems. PLCs, I/O, control architecture
- **Engineering degree** (Electrical, Mechanical, Industrial) or hands-on equivalent
- **One number you can defend in conversation** — quota attainment, YoY growth, a named account win
- **Home office inside your territory**, 30 to 50 percent regional travel depending on the patch
- **Self-directed.** There is a product line and an applications team behind you. There is no script

## Salary and travel

**$80,000 to $105,000 base plus uncapped commission.** OTE $115K–$120K Mountain West; $135K–$150K Northeast; mid-range Midwest and Southeast.

That range is our read on comparable US automation and sensor roles. We name the client's actual band on the first call, before you spend a minute on an interview loop.

Field-based, home office in territory, with regular customer-site travel. This is outside sales, not a remote desk job.
